\begin{abstract}
Current approaches to chemical reservoir computing and prebiotic memory systems systematically conflate the passive retention of physical states with autonomous learning. By post-processing material readouts through external computational layers (e.g., Python-driven linear regressions), existing literature mistakenly attributes agency to matrices that merely exhibit thermal or structural inertia. This paper introduces a rigorous theoretical and experimental framework that decouples "memory" into three distinct, hardware-embedded algorithmic operations: (1) \textit{Hysteresis} ($H$), the passive retention of historical variations; (2) \textit{Conditional Eligibility or the Clutch} ($E \times R$), the gating of biestable state-writing strictly via the alignment of internal receptivity windows with external reinforcement signals; and (3) \textit{Homeostatic Saturation or the Anti-Self} ($S$), an intrinsic mechanical break that halts acquisition to prevent autogenous structural collapse, an evolutionary precursor to the adaptive acquisition mechanics found in CRISPR systems. We define the dynamic coupling law $dM/dt = E \times R \times S$, present three explicit experimental protocols for empirical falsification, and formalize a forensic methodology (``the trace of the non-person'') to analytically decode unique past signal sequences from material structures.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Problem Statement}
In contemporary chemical intelligence literature, a persistent conceptual error blurs the line between a system that is \textit{affected by its history} and a system that \textit{learns from it}. Systems utilizing the formose reaction or complex autocatalytic networks function via thermodynamic dissipation. They store environmental traces because their structural relaxation times are longer than the frequency of incoming stimuli. 

However, extracting these states via mass spectrometry and decoding them through an external neural network or linear script shifts the computational burden onto the observer. The material substrate does not learn; the computer processing the readout does. To establish true material autonomy, the filtering, gating, and termination of informational capture must occur entirely within the intrinsic mechanics of the hardware.

\section{The Tripartite Division of Material Memory}
We formalize material information storage by isolating three mutually independent operations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Hysteresis ($H$):} The baseline capacity of a physical medium to retain geometric, conformational, or concentration alterations induced by past energy inputs. It contains no selective criteria.
    \item \textbf{The Clutch ($E \times R$):} A material AND-gate requiring a temporary internal state of readiness (Eligibility, $E$) to coincide chronologically with an external validation vector (Reward/Reinforcement, $R$). Without coincidence, environmental noise is ignored.
    \item \textbf{The Anti-Self ($S$):} A self-limiting negative feedback mechanism. Just as the primary evolutionary breakthrough of CRISPR was not the acquisition of viral spacers but the automated termination of spacer acquisition to prevent catastrophic auto-immunity, the \textit{Anti-Self} halts structural modification before the memory substrate undergoes irreversible denaturation.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Dynamic Coupling Architecture}
The net structural modification rate ($dM/dt$) of the hardware is governed by the non-linear interaction of its internal gates and external inputs:
\begin{equation}
\frac{dM}{dt} = E(t) \times R(t) \times S(M)
\end{equation}
Where $E(t) \in [0, 1]$ represents the transient photochemically or thermally induced eligibility window, $R(t)$ denotes the localized concentration of catalytic cofactors, and $S(M)$ is an alosteric or steric inhibition function that asymptotically approaches 0 as $M$ approaches a critical threshold $M_{max}$.

\section{Falsifiable Experimental Protocols}
To validate this framework against purely hysteretic reservoirs, three distinct experiments must be performed on the physical hardware:

\subsection{Experiment 1: Clutch Isolation ($\Delta M_{clutch}$)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Protocol:} Apply validation signal $R > 0$ under two control conditions: inside the transient window $E > 0$ (Coincidence) and outside the window $E = 0$ (Decoupled).
    \item \textbf{Critical Metric:} $\Delta M_{clutch} = M_{coincidence} - M_{decoupled}$.
    \item \textbf{Falsification Condition:} If $\Delta M_{clutch} = 0$, the system behaves as a linear accumulator of damage. The Clutch operation does not exist.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Experiment 2: The Anti-Self Limit ($S_{limit}$)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Protocol:} Saturate the system with continuous, high-amplitude validation inputs ($R \to \infty$) while maintaining eligibility ($E = 1$).
    \item \textbf{Critical Metric:} $S_{limit} = \lim_{t \to \infty} M(t)$.
    \item \textbf{Falsification Condition:} If the physical medium undergoes catastrophic degradation or structural collapse without establishing a stable plateau ($dM/dt \to 0$), the system lacks a homeostatic acquisition break.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Experiment 3: Autonomous Noise Immunity ($\eta_{autonomy}$)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Protocol:} Flood the system with stochastic environmental background noise to maximize baseline hysteresis ($H$), then execute the Protocol for Experiment 1.
    \item \textbf{Critical Metric:} $\eta_{autonomy} = \Delta M_{clutch, noise} / \Delta M_{clutch, pristine}$.
    \item \textbf{Falsification Condition:} If $\eta_{autonomy} \to 0$, the internal gating mechanics are enslaved by historical thermodynamic noise, failing the requirement for autonomous learning.
\end{itemize}

\section{The Trace of the Non-Person: Forensic Inference}
Because an autonomous material system gates information capture through strict non-linear conditions, its final configuration $M_{final}$ behaves as an informational bottleneck. We define the forensic trace by calculating the Kullback-Leibler divergence between the material state distribution ($P_{object}$) and the historical statistical properties of its local spatial environment ($P_{local}$):
\begin{equation}
D_{KL}(P_{object} \parallel P_{local}) \gg 0
\end{equation}
Anomalous configurations yield a high divergence, proving that the object could not have been sculpted by local thermodynamic equilibrium. By analytically inverting Equation 1, the precise minimum chronological sequence of multi-variable signals required to construct the state can be computed:
\begin{equation}
\vec{S}_{history} = \{ (E_1, R_1), (E_2, R_2), \dots, (E_n, R_n) \}
\end{equation}
If $\vec{S}_{history}$ demands alternating, highly ordered conditional inputs that are completely absent from the local geological or environmental history, the material artifact stands as a definitive, un-falsifiable record of an extinct selective process or deliberate non-human training vector.
